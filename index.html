About Me

Learn more about my background, experience, and passions.

Hello, my name is Inthuja! I am an aspiring developer and builder working at the intersection of data, science, and software. With a background in biomedical sciences and hands-on experience in product analytics, I’m drawn to problems where careful analysis and thoughtful engineering come together.

My path from the lab to the terminal has shaped how I approach problem-solving: the strongest solutions begin with asking the right questions, then using data and technology to answer them with clarity and intention.

I’m especially interested in building products where insights directly influence what gets created and improved. As I continue expanding my full-stack and data engineering skills, I’m looking for opportunities in product, healthtech, or mission-driven companies where I can contribute across the entire lifecycle: from defining the question to shipping the answer.
